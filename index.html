AP Physics C — Chapter 7: Conservation of Linear Momentum & Collisions

1. Impulse-Momentum Theorem

For any closed mechanical system experiencing net external force F(t) over time interval Δt = t2 − t1, the total impulse equals the vector change in linear momentum:

J = ∫ Fnet dt = Δp = m vf − m vi

2. Elastic vs. Inelastic One-Dimensional Collisions

In an isolated two-body collision with zero external impulse, total linear momentum is strictly conserved across both elastic and inelastic regimes. Kinetic energy is additionally conserved only when internal restitution equals unity:

Collision Type Momentum (Σp) Kinetic Energy (ΣK) Restitution (e)
Perfectly Elastic Conserved Conserved 1.00
Partially Inelastic Conserved Thermal / Acoustic Loss 0 < e < 1
Completely Inelastic Conserved (Shared vf) Maximum Permitted Loss 0.00
